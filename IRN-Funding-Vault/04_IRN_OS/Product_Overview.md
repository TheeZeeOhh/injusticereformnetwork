# IRN OS: Product Overview

The **IRN OS** is the technological backbone of the Criminal Injustice Reform Network. Unlike conventional civic-tech platforms that rely on centralized corporate clouds, the IRN OS is an offline-first, Zero-Trust ecosystem designed for extreme resilience and privacy.

### Core Capabilities
- **Decentralized Syncing:** Utilizes a peer-to-peer Merkle-Search Trie with LWW-CRDTs to ensure data synchronizes securely without a central server.
- **Harm Receipts Generator:** A built-in toolkit for calculating disparate impact, tracking environmental toxicity, and reverse-engineering state risk algorithms.
- **Mutual Aid Engine:** Facilitates decentralized funding pipelines directly to frontline communities.
- **Local AI Integration:** Employs localized Ollama vector embeddings for advanced data analysis and fact-checking, keeping all intelligence on the user's device.

We are building Afro-Futurist architecture: tech that serves the people, protects their data, and scales liberation.
