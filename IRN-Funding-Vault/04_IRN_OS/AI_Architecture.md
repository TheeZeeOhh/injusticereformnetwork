# AI Architecture

## The Philosophy
Corporate AI models are inherently extractive and frequently perpetuate the biases of the state. The IRN OS AI architecture is built on the premise of sovereignty. We bring the intelligence to the data, not the data to the intelligence.

## Implementation
- **Local LLMs:** The platform utilizes quantized, locally-run large language models (via Ollama). 
- **Vector Embeddings:** We generate and store vector embeddings locally, allowing organizers to perform semantic searches over thousands of FOIA documents and state reports without an internet connection.
- **Bias Mitigation:** Our models are specifically fine-tuned and prompted to recognize and reverse-engineer state risk algorithms, identifying proxies used for digital redlining.
