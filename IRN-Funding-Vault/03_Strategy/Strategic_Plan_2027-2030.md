# Strategic Plan (2027-2030)

## The Objective
To scale our offline-first, sovereign civic-tech infrastructure nationwide, dismantle state risk algorithms, and secure environmental justice for marginalized communities.

## Year 1: Infrastructure & Consolidation (2027)
- **Deploy the Soul Walk Mesh:** Establish autonomous, decentralized mesh networks across 10 major metropolitan target areas.
- **Radiant Threshold Expansion:** Upskill an additional 5,000 organizers through our academy, integrating deep InfoSec and data-literacy training.
- **Harm Receipts Rollout:** Beta-test the local AI Fact Checker and FOIA Builder to generate legislative Harm Receipts against environmental redlining.

## Year 2: The Money Matrix & Mutual Aid (2028)
- **Decentralized Funding:** Launch our peer-to-peer mutual aid engine to directly fund grassroots organizers without relying on traditional philanthropic gatekeepers.
- **Youth Campaign Integration:** Bring our Policy School (grades 6-12) fully online using the IRN OS, empowering the next generation of Afro-Futurist leaders.

## Year 3: Systemic Accountability (2029)
- **Algorithm Reverse-Engineering:** Utilize our distributed network of organizers and local AI capabilities to publicly expose and legally challenge 5 major state risk assessment tools.
- **Regional Advocacy Centers:** Establish physical, community-owned hubs fully integrated with our digital mesh, serving as safe havens and strategic war rooms.

## Year 4: Sovereign Liberation (2030)
- **National Scalability:** Reach critical mass with our decentralized infrastructure, rendering state-sponsored surveillance ineffective against our coalition.
- **Policy Overhaul:** Leverage the amassed Harm Receipts and mobilized voting blocks to drive federal and state-level policy changes that protect Black trans futures and mandate environmental equity.
