# Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **State Surveillance / Interception** | High | High | Strict adherence to InfoSec Level 3 protocols. Reliance on Zero-Trust, offline-first mesh networks (Soul Walk) rather than centralized cloud servers. |
| **Funding Shortfalls** | Medium | High | Diversify revenue via decentralized funding models ("The Money Matrix"), secure 501(c)(3) partnerships, and avoid reliance on single philanthropic entities. |
| **Technological Obsolescence** | Low | Medium | Continuous iteration of the IRN OS. Open-sourcing core components to tap into community-driven innovation and security auditing. |
| **Organizer Burnout** | High | Medium | Center "Joy as the Final Word." Implement restorative justice practices internally, mandate rotating leadership, and celebrate all wins. |
| **Legislative Backlash** | Medium | High | Maintain robust, undeniable data (Harm Receipts) to validate all campaigns. Build strong coalitions with aligned legal advocacy groups to defend against hostile policy. |
