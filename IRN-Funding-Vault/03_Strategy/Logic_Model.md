# Logic Model

## Inputs (Resources)
- **Human Capital:** 8,600+ trained organizers, elite civic-tech engineers, visionary leadership (Aziza, BeKura).
- **Technology:** Proprietary IRN OS, local Ollama AI models, Soul Walk mesh networks, decentralized databases (LWW-CRDTs).
- **Financial Capital:** Mutual aid networks, 501(c)(3) partnerships, decentralized funding streams.
- **Data/Knowledge:** Power mapping curricula, environmental toxicity datasets, algorithmic risk assessment research.

## Activities
- **Training & Education:** Operating the Radiant Threshold academy and Youth Policy School to upskill advocates.
- **Tech Deployment:** Distributing offline-first mesh networks and the IRN OS to frontline communities.
- **Data Generation:** Tracking environmental disparities and generating legislative Harm Receipts.
- **Advocacy & Lobbying:** Engaging with policymakers, exposing algorithmic bias, and demanding systemic reform.

## Outputs
- **Trained Advocates:** Number of organizers completing Radiant Threshold certifications.
- **Mesh Nodes:** Number of active, secure local network nodes deployed.
- **Harm Receipts:** Number of data reports generated and submitted to legislative bodies.
- **Campaigns Launched:** Number of grassroots campaigns initiated using the IRN OS.

## Outcomes
- **Short-Term:** Increased data literacy and digital security among organizers; improved coordination of mutual aid.
- **Medium-Term:** Greater public awareness of algorithmic bias and environmental racism; policy drafts introduced based on our Harm Receipts.
- **Long-Term:** Permanent dismantling of punitive state risk algorithms; establishment of thriving, self-governing Afro-Futurist communities free from digital and environmental oppression.
