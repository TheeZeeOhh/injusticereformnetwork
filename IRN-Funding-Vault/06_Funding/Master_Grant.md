# Master Grant Narrative

**Project Title:** Sovereign Infrastructure for Marginalized Communities  
**Organization:** Criminal Injustice Reform Network (CIRN)  
**Total Request:** $5,000,000  

## Statement of Need
Marginalized communities face interconnected crises: mass incarceration, environmental racism, and digital surveillance. Traditional advocacy models lack the technological infrastructure to combat these systemic issues effectively.

## Project Design
We are deploying the IRN OS—an offline-first, decentralized civic-tech platform—across 30 target cities. This grant will fund the engineering, deployment, and the Radiant Threshold training academy required to upskill 10,000 organizers.

## Outcomes
- Deployment of 5,000 secure mesh nodes.
- Generation of 500+ legislative Harm Receipts.
- Direct distribution of $1M via our mutual aid engine.
