# History

Founded by organizers who experienced the limitations of traditional advocacy firsthand, CIRN emerged as a radical response to digital surveillance and systemic oppression. 

- **Phase I (The Awakening):** Initially formed as a coalition of grassroots advocates focusing on local decarceration efforts and regional court-watching.
- **Phase II (The Technological Pivot):** Recognizing that traditional organizing was vulnerable to state interception, CIRN pivoted to integrate advanced civic-tech solutions, laying the groundwork for the IRN OS and the Radiant Threshold training platform.
- **Phase III (Sovereign Expansion):** Expanding our scope to include environmental justice and systemic data analysis, we successfully trained over 8,600 organizers and established a presence in 30+ cities, building out the offline-first mesh networks that protect our operations today.
