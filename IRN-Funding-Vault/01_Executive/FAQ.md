# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the Criminal Injustice Reform Network (CIRN)?**
A: CIRN is a coalition of grassroots organizers and civic technologists dedicated to combating mass incarceration and systemic injustice through the deployment of sovereign, offline-first digital infrastructure and strategic policy advocacy.

**Q: What do you mean by "Sovereign Infrastructure"?**
A: We build tech that does not rely on centralized corporate servers or state surveillance. Our tools utilize Zero-Trust architecture, peer-to-peer syncing (like LWW-CRDTs), and local AI processing to ensure community data remains private, secure, and entirely owned by the users.

**Q: How does CIRN approach environmental justice?**
A: We recognize that environmental racism is inextricably linked to systemic oppression. Our tools empower communities to track toxic outfalls, gather localized data, and build evidence-backed campaigns to hold state and corporate polluters accountable.

**Q: Who leads the organization?**
A: CIRN is led by BeKura Smith (President) and Aziza "Zee" Okoro (Vice President & Strategic Partnerships), operating under a model of rotating leadership, consensus building, and community accountability.

**Q: What is the funding used for?**
A: Capital goes directly toward deploying local mesh networks, funding our Youth Campaign and Policy School, and scaling our digital platforms to support organizers across the country. We prioritize decentralized funding structures that directly benefit marginalized groups.
