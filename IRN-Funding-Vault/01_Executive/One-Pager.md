# CIRN: One-Pager

## Who We Are
The Criminal Injustice Reform Network (CIRN) is a coalition of technologists, advocates, and community leaders building decentralized infrastructure to dismantle systemic oppression.

## Our Core Pillars
1. **InfoSec as Witchcraft (Privacy-First Tech):** Secure, offline-first, local mesh networks that protect organizer data from surveillance.
2. **The Money Matrix (Decentralized Funding):** Rejecting scarcity by facilitating mutual aid and financial sovereignty for marginalized groups.
3. **Organizing as Ritual:** Structuring our tools to support consensus-building, rotating leadership, and true community accountability.
4. **Afro-Futurist Architecture:** Centering Black trans futures in every application, code push, and policy draft we create.
5. **Joy as the Final Word:** Injecting fierce resilience into our problem-solving, acknowledging every win as a revolutionary act.

## What We Build
- **IRN OS:** A peer-to-peer Merkle-Search Trie sync engine for secure, decentralized organizing.
- **Harm Receipts Generator:** Tools to calculate disparate impact and reverse-engineer state risk algorithms for legislative lobbying.
- **Radiant Threshold:** Our premier training platform for 8,600+ activists learning power mapping, coalition building, and campaign strategy.

## Impact & Vision
We are currently operating in 30+ cities, providing the civic-tech backbone for environmental justice and systemic corrections reform. Our vision is a fully autonomous network of empowered communities capable of self-governance, mutual aid, and systemic accountability.
