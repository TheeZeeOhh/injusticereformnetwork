# FOIA Builder Program

The FOIA (Freedom of Information Act) Builder is a core module within the IRN OS designed to democratize access to public records.

## The Problem
State agencies frequently weaponize bureaucracy to delay or deny access to public records, exhausting grassroots organizers.

## The Solution
The FOIA Builder automates the drafting, submission, and tracking of public records requests. It utilizes our local AI to parse complex legal jargon, identify the exact statutes required for compliance, and generate legally unassailable request letters. 

By aggregating FOIA data across our network, we build a decentralized library of state actions, crucial for generating Harm Receipts.
