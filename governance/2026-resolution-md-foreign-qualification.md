## Recitals

**WHEREAS,** Injustice Reform Network (the "Corporation") is a nonstock, nonprofit
corporation duly incorporated under the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia and
recognized as a tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal
Revenue Code (EIN 83-4207890); and

**WHEREAS,** the Corporation conducts and intends to continue conducting
charitable, educational, and civic-accountability activities within the State of
Maryland, including in Baltimore and the greater DMV region; and

**WHEREAS,** the State of Maryland requires an out-of-state ("foreign")
nonprofit corporation transacting business within the State to qualify with the
Maryland State Department of Assessments and Taxation ("SDAT") and obtain a
Certificate of Authority; and

**WHEREAS,** the Board of Directors finds that qualifying to operate in Maryland
preserves the Corporation's original Virginia date of incorporation and its
active federal tax-exempt status, and that failure to qualify prior to
transacting business in Maryland may expose the Corporation to a statutory
penalty of up to $200 and other liabilities; and

**WHEREAS,** the Board has reviewed the estimated costs and process, summarized
in the schedule below;

## Resolutions

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED,** that the Board of Directors hereby
**authorizes and approves** the qualification of the Corporation as a foreign
nonstock/nonprofit corporation in the State of Maryland (the "Foreign
Qualification"); and be it

**FURTHER RESOLVED,** that the Board authorizes the officers of the Corporation
to:

1. Obtain a **Certificate of Good Standing** (Certificate of Existence) from the
   Commonwealth of Virginia, dated within sixty (60) days of the Maryland
   submission;
2. Designate and engage a **Maryland Resident Agent** with a physical Maryland
   street address, whether an individual affiliated with the Corporation or a
   commercial registered-agent service;
3. File the **Foreign Corporation Qualification** with Maryland SDAT via the
   Maryland Business Express portal, designating the entity as **Non-Stock /
   Non-Profit**, and to elect **expedited processing**;
4. Submit, following approval, the **Exempt Organization Fund-Raising Notice** to
   the Maryland Secretary of State (Charities Division) and register on the
   **Maryland Tax Connect** portal for applicable state tax exemptions;

and be it

**FURTHER RESOLVED,** that the Board authorizes expenditure of Corporation funds
not to exceed **$______** to complete the foregoing filings, inclusive of state
fees, expedited-service fees, resident-agent costs, and the Virginia
good-standing certificate; and be it

**FURTHER RESOLVED,** that ______________________ [name/title] is designated as
the authorized signatory and responsible officer to execute all documents and
take all actions reasonably necessary to effect this Resolution, and to report
completion to the Board.
