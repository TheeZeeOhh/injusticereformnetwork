About BeKura Waliah Shabazz

BeKura Waliah Shabazz is the Founder of the Injustice Reform Network (IRN), anchoring the network's VA/757 operations. She is a powerhouse legal advocate, policy strategist, and grassroots organizer dedicated to challenging carceral systems and driving institutional accountability.

As the founder of First State Advocates, BeKura has spent over fifteen years working at the intersection of civil rights, criminal justice reform, and foster system advocacy. She is renowned for building strong coalitions, drafting formal community records demands, and leading campaigns that hold municipal and institutional actors accountable.

Her courses at Radiant Threshold focus on equipping families and communities to challenge state-sponsored neglect and abuse with verified, community-controlled documentation and systemic policy demands.
