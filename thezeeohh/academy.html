Our Approach

Proven Frameworks. Frontline Experience.

The Academy blends the best of established organizing methodology with IRN's decade of frontline work in Hampton Roads, Baltimore, and the broader Mid-Atlantic region. We don't teach theory for theory's sake — every framework in our curriculum has been stress-tested in real campaigns.

Our pedagogy is grounded in adult learning principles, peer practice, and reflection cycles. You don't just watch — you do. Every module includes hands-on exercises, live case studies from IRN's own campaigns, and downloadable tools you can use the same week.
