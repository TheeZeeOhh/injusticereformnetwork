About Marcus Webb

Marcus Webb has spent two decades organizing low-income communities across the American South and Midwest. He's led campaigns that resulted in the release of wrongfully convicted individuals, shut down illegal dumping sites in environmental racism hotspots, and won living wage ordinances in three cities — campaigns that directly improved the lives of tens of thousands of people.

Marcus began organizing at 19 as a tenant rights advocate in Birmingham, Alabama. Over the next two decades he became one of the most respected community organizers in the country — building coalitions across racial, political, and geographic lines that would have seemed impossible. His approach is rooted in deep listening, relentless base-building, and an absolute refusal to accept that things can't change.

In 2018, Marcus founded the People's Power Institute, a national training organization that has equipped over 18,000 organizers with the tools they need to win. He brought that same curriculum to Radiant Threshold to reach activists and advocates who can't access in-person training — making frontline organizing knowledge available everywhere, at any time.

Marcus's teaching philosophy is simple: organizing is a craft, and like any craft, it can be learned. His courses combine theory with real case studies from actual campaigns — including ones he ran, won, and lost. Students leave not just with knowledge, but with tools they can use the next day.
