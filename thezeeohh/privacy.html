Privacy Policy

We built Radiant Threshold on a simple principle: your data belongs to you. No surveillance. No third-party selling. No tracking outside your own device.

Plain-language summary: We don't sell your data. We don't track you across the web. Most of your data never leaves your own device. This policy explains what little we do handle — and why.

1. Who We Are

Radiant Threshold is a training platform operated by the Injustice Reform Network (IRN), a community-led organization based in Baltimore, Maryland. Our mission is to build movement power through accessible, practitioner-led education for organizers, advocates, and community builders.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: IRNVP@pm.me

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the platform:

  • Session booking requests — when you book a strategy session with an instructor, you voluntarily submit your name and email. This is sent directly via email to our VP inbox and is not stored on any server we control.
  • Scholarship applications — name, email, and organizing focus submitted through the scholarship portal. Sent directly via email and stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB.
  • Email communications — if you contact us at IRNVP@pm.me, we retain that correspondence for operational purposes only.
  • Newsletter subscriptions — if you subscribe, your email is stored by our email service provider for the sole purpose of sending you updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Data We Don't Collect

We are explicit about what we do not do:

  • We do not use advertising trackers, pixels, or behavioral profiling tools
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party
  • We do not build user profiles or track you across other websites
  • We do not require account registration to access course content
  • We do not log or store your video or audio from War Room sessions — Jitsi sessions are peer-to-peer and ephemeral
  • We do not store your AI chat history — Amina conversations exist only in your browser session memory
  • We do not collect payment information — Bitcoin and other payments are processed entirely outside our infrastructure

4. Local-First Architecture

Radiant Threshold is designed around a local-first, zero-server model. The following data is stored exclusively in your browser and never transmitted to us:

  • Course exercise answers and session notes (IndexedDB — SovereignDB)
  • Scholarship application vouchers and verification tokens
  • Your exported Sovereign Manifesto files
  • Your referral code and payout method preferences
  • Your Gemini API key (sessionStorage only — cleared when you close the tab)
  • Dashboard draftpad content
You own this data. You can export it, delete it, or purge it at any time from the Dashboard → Settings → Sovereign Ledger panel. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it if you delete it.

5. Communications

When you email us or submit a booking or scholarship form, you are initiating direct contact. We will respond to your inquiry and retain the correspondence for operational continuity. We will never add you to a marketing list without your explicit opt-in.

All IRN communications are handled through Proton Mail (IRNVP@pm.me), which provides end-to-end encryption for email. We chose this specifically because we take communications security seriously for the organizing communities we serve.

6. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of third-party services to operate the platform. Each is disclosed here with its purpose:

  • Jitsi Meet (meet.jit.si) — peer-to-peer video conferencing in the War Room. Sessions are ephemeral. Jitsi's own privacy policy applies to your use of their service.
  • Google Gemini API — powers the Amina AI assistant. Your messages are sent to Google's API using your own API key, which you provide and which is never stored on our servers. Google's API terms apply.
  • Font Awesome (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — icon fonts loaded via CDN. Cloudflare may log standard web request data per their privacy policy.
  • QR Server (api.qrserver.com) — generates QR codes for Bitcoin payment flows. No personal data is included in QR requests.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server access logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any advertising network SDKs.

7. Cookies & Browser Storage

We do not use tracking cookies. We use the following browser storage mechanisms strictly for platform functionality:

  • localStorage — stores your referral code, payout preferences, and any settings you configure. Persists until you clear it.
  • sessionStorage — stores your Gemini API key for the current browser session only. Cleared automatically when you close the tab.
  • IndexedDB (SovereignDB) — stores your course notes, scholarship vouchers, and manifesto data locally. You control and can purge this at any time.

None of these storage mechanisms are shared with or readable by third parties.

8. Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located, we honor the following rights:

  • Right to access — you can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you by emailing IRNVP@pm.me
  • Right to deletion — you can request we delete any correspondence or contact data we hold. Local browser data is under your full control via the Dashboard purge tool.
  • Right to portability — your local data can be exported at any time via Dashboard → Settings → Encrypted Backup Export.
  • Right to opt out — if you subscribed to our newsletter, every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us to be removed.

Because most of your data never reaches us, we cannot fulfill deletion requests for data that exists only in your local browser. Use the Dashboard purge tool to delete that data yourself — instantly and completely.

9. Children's Privacy

Radiant Threshold is intended for users 16 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has submitted personal information to us, please contact us at IRNVP@pm.me and we will promptly delete it.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted in our newsletter. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We will never retroactively change this policy to permit data collection or sharing practices that this version prohibits without clear advance notice and an opportunity to opt out.
