Case Focus

Andrea Balcer & Prisoner Privacy

The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) has launched an investigation into Maine's housing of transgender inmates. They issued an administrative subpoena for the private medical and psychological records of Andrea Balcer, a transgender woman housed at the Maine Correctional Center.

Maine's DOC has refused to comply, citing HIPAA (health privacy) and PREA (Prison Rape Elimination Act) requirements. IRN stands with the privacy defense—because if the federal government can subpoena private psychological records of incarcerated people, no one's data is safe.
