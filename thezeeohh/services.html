Support & Navigation — Legal, Educational & Community

Support That Builds Power

Beyond investigation and training, IRN helps you navigate the legal system, connects you with licensed counsel and trusted partners, and stands with you through court, advocacy, and community organizing. Support is offered on a sliding, no-barrier basis — access is a right, not a privilege.

IRN is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal representation. We provide information, navigation, and advocacy support, and we connect you with licensed attorneys where legal advice or representation is needed. No attorney–client relationship is formed through this website or by using our services.

01
Record & Case Relief
Clearing records, reducing burdens, and reopening closed doors

Expungement Navigation

Understand whether your arrests, charges, or convictions may be eligible for expungement, and get connected with licensed counsel and partners who can help you pursue it — so old records are less likely to block housing, employment, or opportunity.

Record Sealing Navigation

Learn about sealing records from public view where full expungement isn't available, and connect with legal help to file — limiting who can access your history.

Post-Conviction Support

Understand possible options after a conviction — appeals, sentence modifications, and other remedies — and get connected with licensed attorneys who can evaluate and pursue them.

Garnishment Support

Understand your rights around wage and bank garnishments and connect with legal help to challenge collection that may be unlawful or excessive.

Community Service Coordination

Placement and documentation support for court-ordered community service, tracked and verified through IRN partners.

Service In Lieu of Fines & Costs

Learn about options to convert court fines, fees, and costs into community service hours when payment creates hardship, and get connected with legal help to request it.

02
Court & Case Navigation
Standing with you through every court system

Criminal Court Navigation

Plain-language information about the criminal process — arraignment, hearings, and trial — and connection to licensed counsel for advice on your specific case and decisions.

Civil Court Navigation

Information and organizing support for civil matters — housing, debt, family, and small claims — including understanding deadlines and preparing, with referrals to legal help.

Administrative Hearing Navigation

Preparation and referral support for agency and administrative hearings — from benefits appeals to licensing and regulatory actions — connecting you with counsel or authorized advocates where representation is needed.

Case Preparation Support

Help organizing your facts, evidence, and documents so you're ready to work with an attorney — and connection to legal help to evaluate and file your claim.

03
Education & Disability Advocacy
Protecting the right to learn and be supported

Educational Planning & Advocacy

Advocacy for students facing discipline, denial of services, or barriers to enrollment — building a plan that protects their future.

IDEA Accompaniment

Lay-advocate accompaniment and support through IEP and 504 meetings under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, helping you understand the process and the school's obligations.

04
Policy & Systems Change
Fixing the systems, not just the symptoms

Policy Work

Research, drafting, and advocacy for legislation and local ordinances that advance justice and accountability.

Systems Change Work

Long-term campaigns targeting the root structures — courts, agencies, and institutions — that produce recurring harm.

Civic Engagement Education

Community education on rights, voting, public process, and how to hold power accountable between elections.

05
Environmental & Economic Justice
Justice you can breathe, eat, and live in

Food Desert Reduction

Methodologies and partnerships that expand food access in underserved neighborhoods — gardens, co-ops, and distribution networks.

Environmental Advocacy

Fighting environmental harms — air, water, and industrial violations — through complaints, monitoring, and community testimony.
