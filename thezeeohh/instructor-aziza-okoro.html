About Aziza Okoro

Aziza "Zee" Okoro is the Vice President of the Injustice Reform Network (IRN). Based in Baltimore, she is a veteran community organizer and abolitionist strategist dedicated to building non-carceral safety nets that center Trans and Gender-Expansive People of Color.

For over two decades, Aziza has worked on the ground in Baltimore leading campaigns across housing rights, harm reduction, peer support networks, and community-led workforce development. When she isn't leading IRN, she works as a warehouse associate at Home Depot store DFC 5829, grounding her activism in the lived reality of working-class communities.

Aziza's work is driven by a deep commitment to radical truth, absolute solidarity, and systemic power-building.
