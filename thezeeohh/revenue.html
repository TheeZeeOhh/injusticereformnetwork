How it Works

At Radiant Threshold, we believe creators, movement builders, and advocates should be fairly compensated for their knowledge and educational expertise. That's why we maintain the most competitive payout ratio in the social activism and learning space.

You set your price, design your syllabus, and publish your course. When learners register, the revenue is split immediately and credited to your instructor account.

What the 15% Covers

We handle all administrative overhead so you can focus entirely on teaching and building content:

  • Merchant accounts and payment gateway transaction fees
  • Global hosting and secure HD video delivery infra
  • Sovereign group channels, workspace features, and real-time chat relays
  • Marketing campaigns and community outreach initiatives

Revenue & Payout FAQ

When do payouts happen?
Payouts are processed on the 1st of every calendar month for all course registrations completed in the previous month.
What payment methods can I use to receive payouts?
You can receive payouts via direct deposit (Stripe Connect), PayPal, Cash App, or directly to a Bitcoin address (via On-Chain or Lightning Network).
Are there setup fees or monthly hosting fees?
No. There are zero upfront costs, subscription fees, or storage charges to teach on Radiant Threshold. We only succeed when you succeed.
Can I offer discount coupons or scholarships?
Yes. You can generate custom discount codes, free-access tokens, and participate in our sponsored Sovereign Scholarship program where community organizations fund seats for learners.
