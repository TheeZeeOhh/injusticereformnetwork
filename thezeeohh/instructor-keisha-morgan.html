About Dr. Keisha Morgan

Dr. Keisha Morgan is an environmental justice scholar, grassroots strategist, and community science advocate. She has spent fifteen years working alongside frontline communities to challenge environmental racism and industrial polluters.

Dr. Morgan spent over a decade coordinating advocacy campaigns in Louisiana's "Cancer Alley," leading community-led science and data tracking programs, and advising Indigenous groups on water rights protection.

Her courses at Radiant Threshold provide practical tools for frontline environmental campaigns — teaching how to collect verifiable community data, challenge corporate polluters in regulatory hearings, and build local climate action plans.
