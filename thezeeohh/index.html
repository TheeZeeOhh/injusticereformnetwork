Why Radiant Threshold

Built for Those Who Fight for Real

Radiant Threshold is the premier training platform for activists, organizers, advocates, and changemakers. We believe knowledge is power — and power builds movements.

Frontline Instructors

Learn from people who've actually organized campaigns, argued in court, and changed laws — not just academics. Every instructor has done the work you're training for.

Access for All

Sliding scale pricing, full scholarships, and free courses ensure money never blocks access to movement knowledge. The "Know Your Rights" course is always free, no exceptions.

Live Strategy Sessions

Monthly live cohorts, Q&As with organizers, and real-time campaign support for active movements. You're not just taking a course — you're joining a network.

Legal Defense Resources

Know your rights before you need them. State-by-state guides, arrest protocols, and access to movement lawyers — included with relevant courses.

Organizer Network

Join our growing network of organizers. Share tactics, coordinate campaigns, and find coalition partners in your city.

Movement Credentials

Earn verified credentials recognized by coalitions, nonprofits, and advocacy organizations. Prove your training and advance your role in the movement.

Why IRN Built This

The system isn't broken.
It was built this way.

The Injustice Reform Network was founded because people kept getting hurt by systems they didn't understand and couldn't fight. Corrupt officials. Abusive police. Landlords who knew the law and counted on you not to. Courts designed for lawyers.

We built Radiant Threshold because training was the missing piece. Not awareness — there's plenty of that. But the actual skills: how to organize your neighbors, how to run a campaign, how to request public records, how to tell a story that moves people to act.

This isn't a training company that stumbled into activism. We're a movement that built a training platform because the movement needed it.
