About Amara Osei

Amara Osei, J.D., is a civil rights attorney and legal educator dedicated to defending constitutional rights and supporting grassroots movements. She has spent over twelve years representing protesters, activists, and marginalized communities.

Amara has defended over 400 demonstrators, won landmark cases protecting First Amendment rights at public demonstrations, and established Know Your Rights training programs used by bail funds and organizer networks in 22 states.

Her teaching is focused on practical, actionable legal defense — ensuring that organizers, legal observers, and activists understand their rights and how to protect themselves and their networks before, during, and after direct actions.
