    What this is, and what it isn't

    Hours are mutual credit. They cannot be bought with money and cannot be redeemed for money, which is what keeps this out of money-transmission territory. The ledger is the whole mechanism: a balance is a record of what the commons owes you, held by the commons.

    The aid fund records disbursements IRN makes from money it already holds. It does not move anyone's money for them.

    Person-to-person payment is a different build with a licensed partner behind it. It carries identity verification and reporting duties that would contaminate everything on this page, so it does not belong in this file.
